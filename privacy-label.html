App Privacy

Current release-candidate summary · August 11, 2026

Data Not Collected. Trelka is designed as a local-first app. In the current audited release candidate, Ocoee Studios LLC does not receive your property records, seller details, photos, notes, tasks, agent profile, or reports through an app backend, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cloud-sync service.

What stays on your device

  • Property and seller details you enter
  • Room notes, statuses, tasks, and suggestions you save
  • Property photos and optional agent/profile images
  • Agent and brokerage profile information
  • Generated seller preparation PDF reports
  • Local app settings and records

When information can leave Trelka

Information can leave the app when you deliberately choose to send, share, save, print, or export it. Examples include:

  • Email Seller: prepares the seller report in the iOS system mail composer. If a valid seller email is stored for that property, it may be prefilled. You review the message and choose whether to send it.
  • Share PDF: opens Apple's share sheet so you can choose a destination.
  • Print / Save to Files: sends the report to the system destination you select.
  • JSON export: creates a local export that you decide where to save or share.
  • Contact Support: opens an email to Ocoee Studios support; you control the message and whether it is sent.

These user-directed destinations may have their own privacy practices. Trelka does not silently upload this content to Ocoee Studios.

Tracking and advertising

The audited Trelka release candidate contains no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, behavioral analytics, attribution SDK, or data-broker sharing. It does not use data for targeted advertising.

Permissions

Trelka may ask for Camera and Photo Library access when you use features that need them. Those permissions support local property-photo and profile-image workflows. Trelka does not request your location, contacts, microphone, or health data for its current feature set.

App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

For the currently audited release candidate, our recommended App Store Connect answers are:

  • Does this app collect data? No
  • Data used to track you? No
  • Data linked to you? None declared because the developer/integrated partners do not collect app data in this release candidate

This summary is version-specific. If Trelka adds a purchase SDK, accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or another feature that changes data flows, we will review and update the App Store disclosures before that build ships.
