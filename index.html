Trelka
Pre-listing walkthrough and seller preparation for real-estate agents.

Walk the property. Build the plan.

Trelka helps agents run a room-by-room pre-listing walkthrough, capture photos and notes, build a preparation task list, and hand the seller a clear plan as a PDF report.

Get Support Read Privacy Policy

Room-by-room walkthroughs

Move through the property one room at a time, tracking which rooms are reviewed, in progress, or still to do.

Preparation tasks

Build a preparation task list for each room, with priority and effort, drawn from suggestions or written yourself.

Photos and notes

Photograph rooms with the camera or import from your photo library, and keep notes alongside the rooms they belong to.

Seller-plan PDF reports

Generate a seller preparation report as a PDF, with your agent profile and brokerage details, and share it when you are ready.

Local-first design

Trelka stores the properties, photos, notes, and profile details you enter on your device. It has no user accounts and no cloud sync.

No advertising

Trelka contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics, and no cross-app tracking.

A workflow tool, not professional advice

Trelka's suggestions and reports are informational workflow aids. They are not legal, appraisal, valuation, inspection, engineering, contracting, or financial advice, and Trelka does not guarantee any particular sale, appraisal, inspection, or timing outcome. See the Terms of Use.
