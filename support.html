Common questions

Trelka cannot use my camera

Trelka asks for camera access the first time you photograph a room. If you declined, open the iOS Settings app, find Trelka, and turn on Camera. If camera access stays off you can still add photos by importing them from your photo library, and the rest of the app — rooms, notes, tasks, and reports — continues to work without photos.

Trelka cannot see my photo library

Photo access is requested when you import a property photo, headshot, or brokerage logo. In the iOS Settings app, select Trelka and choose the level of Photos access you want. If you selected limited access, iOS shows only the photos you specifically allowed; you can change that selection from the same screen.

How do I generate and share a seller report?

Open a property and choose its report. Trelka builds a PDF from the rooms, notes, photos, and preparation tasks you recorded, along with the agent profile and brokerage details from your profile. Review the report before sending it, then use the share option to hand it off through the iOS share sheet — mail, messages, AirDrop, Files, or another app. Once shared, that copy is managed by wherever you sent it.

How do I remove the demo property?

The demo property is sample content for exploring the app, and it is separate from the properties you create. You can remove it on its own without affecting your real work. If you want it back later, you can load it again.

Where is my data stored?

Trelka is local-first. Your properties, rooms, notes, tasks, photos, and profile are stored on your device, and there are no accounts and no cloud sync. Nothing is sent to Ocoee Studios. Data leaves the app only when you share a report, export your data, or use an Apple service such as device backup. Deleting Trelka removes its local container, subject to your backup settings. See Privacy Choices.

Can I move my data to another device?

Because Trelka has no cloud sync, your records live on the device that created them. Settings includes a data export that writes your property records, rooms, tasks, seller details, and agent profile to a JSON file you can share or save. Photo image files are not included in that export. A device backup or device-to-device transfer through Apple is what carries the full app container to a new phone.

Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Email support with the steps that produced the problem, what you expected, what happened instead, and any screenshot. Feature requests are welcome too — tell us what you were trying to accomplish. We read everything that comes in, though we are a small studio and cannot commit to a specific response time.
