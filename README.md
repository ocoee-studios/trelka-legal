# Trelka Legal

Official public website, support information, and legal pages for **Trelka**, published by **Ocoee Studios LLC**.

Trelka is a privacy-first iPhone and Apple Watch app for real estate agents. It helps agents prepare properties for market, manage the work around their day, capture notes and follow-ups, and create seller-ready plans while keeping working data local to the device.

- Privacy Policy — `privacy.html`
